## The Idea

Content creators and thought leaders spend hours every week:

1. Reading articles across many sources
2. Synthesizing insights
3. Crafting LinkedIn posts, tweets, or newsletters from that research

SparkFeed will automate the bridge between **reading** and **content creation**.

## The Core Workflow

```txt frame="none"
Read 10 blogs → Select relevant articles → AI generates a post → Edit & publish
```

Here's a concrete example:

**You read these 5 articles this week:**
- "The death of third-party cookies" (TechCrunch)
- "First-party data strategies for 2026" (Marketing Week)
- "How Safari handles user tracking" (WebKit Blog)
- "Advertisers shift to contextual targeting" (Digiday)
- "Privacy regulations tighten in the EU" (Reuters)

**SparkFeed generates this LinkedIn post:**

> 🔒 The cookie era is ending. Here's what you need to know:
>
> Third-party cookies in Chrome are officially deprecated. Advertisers who relied on cross-site tracking are scrambling to adapt.
>
> The winners? Brands with strong first-party data strategies. If you've been building direct relationships with your audience, this is your moment.
>
> Key takeaways:
> → Contextual targeting is back and more sophisticated than ever
> → EU regulations are accelerating the timeline globally
> → Safari already blocks 99% of third-party trackers
>
> The privacy-first web is here. Ready or not.
>
> #MarTech #Privacy #DigitalMarketing

## Post Formats

The generator will support multiple content formats:

| Format | Platform | Style |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **LinkedIn post** | LinkedIn | Professional, insight-led, 150–300 words |
| **Thread** | X (Twitter) | Punchy, numbered points, under 280 chars each |
| **Newsletter paragraph** | Substack / Beehiiv | Editorial voice, conversational |
| **Key takeaways** | Any | Bulleted summary, 5–7 points |
| **Executive brief** | Internal use | Formal, concise, business-focused |

## How to Trigger Generation

The planned UX will work like this:

1. Select articles in the article list (checkboxes)
2. Click **"Generate Post"** in the toolbar
3. Choose the format (LinkedIn, Thread, etc.)
4. Review and edit the AI-generated draft
5. Copy to clipboard or share directly (where API access is available)

## Quality Controls

The AI post generator will include guardrails:

- **Source attribution:** Always cites the original articles it drew from
- **Fact preservation:** Doesn't invent statistics or quote fabrications
- **Tone selector:** Choose Professional, Conversational, or Provocative
- **Length control:** Specify target word count

<Aside type="tip">
  The best workflow is to curate your feeds around a specific niche, let SparkFeed surface the most important articles, and use the generator as a **first draft**, not a final product. Your editing and perspective are what make the content valuable.
</Aside>

## Privacy Considerations

If using a local AI provider (Ollama), all generation happens on your machine, so your article content is never transmitted. If using a cloud API (OpenAI, Anthropic), article content is sent to that provider per their terms of service.
