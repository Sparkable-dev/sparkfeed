## What is Trend Detection?

Trend Detection analyzes articles across all your feeds and identifies **topics that appear repeatedly** in a short period of time.

If 10 articles across your feeds mention "Model Context Protocol" in the same week, that's a signal. It's becoming important in your niche.

## How It Works

The detection pipeline will:

1. **Extract topics** from new articles using keyword extraction or NLP
2. **Track frequency:** how often each topic appears across feeds and over time
3. **Score velocity:** topics growing rapidly in mentions score higher
4. **Surface emerging trends:** notify you through a dedicated "Trending" section

## The "Trending Now" View

A new section in the sidebar will show your personalized trending topics:

```txt frame="none"

  2. SQLite in production          ██████    11 mentions
  3. React Server Components       █████     9 mentions

  5. Distributed SQLite (Turso)    ███       5 mentions
```

These trends are derived *only* from your own feeds. No external data, no "what's trending on the internet."

## Why This Matters

Standard RSS reading is chronological. Trend detection adds a **topic layer**:

- **Spot emerging technologies** before they're mainstream
- **Identify consensus:** when many smart people write about the same thing, pay attention
- **Plan your content:** trend data tells you what your audience cares about right now
- **Reduce FOMO:** confidence that you haven't missed anything important

## Personalization

Because trends are computed from *your* feeds, they're uniquely relevant to your interests. A developer following tech blogs will see different trends than a marketer following advertising publications.

<Aside type="note">
  Trend Detection works best when you follow 15+ feeds. With more sources, the signal-to-noise ratio improves significantly.
</Aside>
