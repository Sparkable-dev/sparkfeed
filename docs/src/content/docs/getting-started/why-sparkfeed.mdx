---
title: Why Sparkfeed
description: The problem Sparkfeed solves and why it is self-hostable.
---

Algorithmic feeds decide what to show and make research hard to reproduce. Sparkfeed gives a workspace an explicit source list and keeps the collected articles under the operator's control.

Sparkfeed can follow normal feeds and monitor sites without RSS. The same workspace can be read by people, queried through REST, or connected to an AI client through MCP.

Community Edition contains the complete product. A self-hoster supplies PostgreSQL, email, and optional AI credentials. Sparkable's commercial service sells operation and support rather than a closed capability set.
