# Search traffic snapshot

**Domains:** sparkable.dev, sparkframe.dev, sparkfeed.dev  
**Run date:** 2026-08-31  
**Dataset:** Google, United States, English  
**Requested period:** past 30 days  
**Available period:** DataForSEO August 2026 monthly estimate

## Executive read

DataForSEO detects only a very small amount of Google search traffic across the three domains. Sparkable has the strongest footprint, with two US ranking keywords and roughly 2.7 modeled organic clicks for August. Its clickstream model gives a slightly higher estimate of 5.0 visits. Sparkfeed has one low ranking keyword and an estimated 0.15 organic clicks. Sparkframe has no detected ranking keywords or search traffic in the US dataset.

These numbers are modeled search estimates, not observed visits. They exclude direct, referral, social, email, and most other traffic. Use Google Analytics, Search Console, Cloudflare Analytics, or server logs for exact 30-day traffic.

## Important points

1. **Sparkable is the only domain with measurable branded demand.** It ranks fourth for `sparkable`, which supplies most of its current estimated search traffic.
2. **Sparkable's clearest organic opportunity is the Astro comparison page.** It ranks around page six for a keyword with 390 monthly US searches. Moving that page into the top 20 would matter more than the current branded movement.
3. **Sparkfeed has started indexing for a relevant non-brand query.** The ranking for `website to rss` is weak at position 62, but it confirms that Google associates the article with the intended topic.
4. **Sparkframe is below DataForSEO's detection threshold.** This does not prove the site had zero visitors. It means DataForSEO found no measurable Google ranking footprint in its US English database.
5. **The standard traffic model did not grow from July to August.** Sparkable's clickstream estimate rose from 1.867 to 4.964, but the volume is too small to call that meaningful growth.
6. **No paid-search visibility was detected for any domain.** No featured-snippet or local-pack traffic was returned either.

## Method

Data came from DataForSEO Labs Historical Rank Overview, Domain Rank Overview, Ranked Keywords, Relevant Pages, Bulk Traffic Estimation, and Backlinks Summary. All domains used the same Google US English settings. Similar-keyword deduplication was checked both on and off because it incorrectly removed Sparkable's branded keyword from one bulk total. The cleaned comparison uses the historical August series for month-level traffic and the non-deduplicated keyword dataset for ranking details.
